# Saturn Protocol Spec

The spec is versioned independently of any implementation. Implementations declare the spec version they conform to via the TXT `version` field.

| Spec | Status | TXT `version` | Documents |
|---|---|---|---|
| **v0.2** | Draft | `1` | [Proposal](v0.2/proposal.md) · [Wire format](v0.2/wire-format.md) |
| v0.1 | Superseded | `1` | (folded into v0.2) |

Stability markers used in spec documents:

- **Stable** — implementations MAY rely on the field across spec versions.
- **Draft** — subject to change before promotion to Stable.
- **Deprecated** — scheduled for removal in a future spec version.
