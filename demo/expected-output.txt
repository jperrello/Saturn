Notes:
- A "[Errno 9] Bad file descriptor" line in the discover output is a
  cosmetic warning from the userspace mDNS backend during shutdown of
  the discovery socket. Discovery still succeeds. Tracked separately
  from this demo.
- "models: none" in the TXT record is expected: Saturn does not
  pre-enumerate Ollama's local model list into the mDNS TXT record.
  The chat completion still routes correctly because the model name
  is in the request body, not the discovery record.
